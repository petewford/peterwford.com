About

Amsterdam, NL

I'm Pete, a senior product designer with nearly a decade of experience. I specialise in end-to-end product discovery, experimentation, and turning complex business problems into clear, high-impact experiences.

At Angi I've been helping transform the home services industry, connecting homeowners with trusted pros across Europe and North America.

Before that I was the sole designer at Add to Event, the UK's largest events marketplace. I led all design output, from the UX of both sides of the marketplace to marketing assets, and built and maintained the public site.

Fresh out of university I joined ASOS, working across the retention stream on complex fashion and logistics problems. Before that, a placement year at IBM iX, bringing Watson AI and blockchain into projects for clients like Centrica, First Direct and Vodafone.
